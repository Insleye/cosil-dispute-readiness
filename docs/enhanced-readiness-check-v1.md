# Cosil Enhanced Dispute Readiness Check

## Content, Scoring and Gating Spec — FINAL (for development handoff)

Aligned with: Marketing to Money workbook commercial framework (ascension, trust-building, category of one) and Cosil positioning (no legal advice, solicitor-equivalent judgement, clarity/control/proportion, no overt selling).

## 1. Product summary

**Price:** £24.99, one-off, self-serve

**Purpose:** Answers "how ready am I to deal with this dispute?" — not "what should I do about it?"

**Boundary:** Diagnostic and reflective only. Never advises on evidence to gather, correspondence to send, routes to pursue, legal merits, or settlement position. Those remain Cosil's paid professional services.

**Funnel position:** Free content → **£24.99 Enhanced Dispute Readiness Check** → £39/month Membership and/or From £395 Strategy Consultation → Bespoke services. This replaces the existing free Readiness Check entirely.

## 2. Six dimensions (locked)

| Dimension | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| Issue & Position | Clarity about what is actually disputed |
| Evidence & Information | Completeness and organisation of available information |
| Exposure & Stakes | Awareness of what may be affected |
| Actions & Escalation | Understanding of what has happened and how far matters have progressed |
| Options Awareness | Awareness of broad dispute-management routes |
| Preparedness | Overall ability to make an informed next decision |

Pressure-tested against: leasehold/service charge disputes, RMC governance disputes, commercial contract/payment disputes. All six hold without distortion.

## 3. Scoring architecture (locked)

- 24 scored questions, 4 per dimension
- Each scored internally: 0 = Not established / 1 = Partly established / 2 = Established
- Customer never sees numerical scores
- Dimension max: 8

| Score | Band |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 | Needs Attention |
| 3–5 | Developing |
| 6–8 | Established |

No overall percentage or single score. Output is the six-dimension profile only.

## 4. The 24 questions (final)

### Dimension 1: Issue & Position

**Q1.1** How clearly could you summarise, in a few sentences, what the dispute is actually about?

- Not clearly at all (0)
- I could give a general idea, but not a precise summary (1)
- Very clearly (2)

**Q1.2** Do you understand what the other party's position or view of the situation is, even if you disagree with it?

- No, I don't know their position (0)
- I have some idea, but I'm not fully sure (1)
- Yes, I understand their position (2)

**Q1.3** Are you clear on what outcome you are actually seeking from this situation?

- No, I haven't defined this (0)
- I have a rough idea (1)
- Yes, this is clear to me (2)

**Q1.4** Do you know which specific points are agreed between you and the other party, and which are genuinely in dispute?

- No, this isn't clear (0)
- Partly, some points are clear and others aren't (1)
- Yes, this is clear (2)

### Dimension 2: Evidence & Information

**Q2.1** Do you have the key documents relevant to this situation (e.g. contracts, correspondence, notices) in one place?

- No, they are scattered or missing (0)
- Some are gathered, others aren't (1)
- Yes, they are gathered together (2)

**Q2.2** Could you currently put together a clear timeline of the key events in order?

- No (0)
- I could attempt one, but it would have gaps (1)
- Yes (2)

**Q2.3** Do you have written records (letters, emails, messages) covering the main points of disagreement?

- No, mostly verbal or undocumented (0)
- Some written records exist (1)
- Yes, the main points are documented (2)

**Q2.4** If someone else needed to understand this situation from your records alone, could they do so?

- No, they'd need a lot explained to them (0)
- They could get a partial picture (1)
- Yes, largely (2)

### Dimension 3: Exposure & Stakes

**Q3.1** Have you identified what could be financially affected if this situation continues or escalates?

- No (0)
- I have a rough sense (1)
- Yes, clearly (2)

**Q3.2** Have you considered how this situation could affect relationships (e.g. with a neighbour, tenant, business contact, board, or colleague)?

- No, I haven't thought about this (0)
- I've thought about it briefly (1)
- Yes, I've considered this carefully (2)

**Q3.3** Have you considered any time-related consequences, such as delays, deadlines, or ongoing disruption?

- No (0)
- Somewhat (1)
- Yes (2)

**Q3.4** Have you considered any wider consequences beyond the immediate issue, such as reputation, precedent, or future decisions this could affect?

- No (0)
- I've thought about this a little (1)
- Yes, I've considered this (2)

### Dimension 4: Actions & Escalation

**Q4.1** Are you clear about what actions or communications have already taken place in this dispute?

- No, not clearly (0)
- Partly (1)
- Yes, clearly (2)

**Q4.2** Have you already sent or received communication that sets out a firm position (rather than an open discussion)?

- Yes, and positions feel fixed already (0)
- Some firm positions have been stated (1)
- No, the situation is still open, or positions remain flexible (2)

**Q4.3** Are you aware of any deadlines or time limits that already apply to this situation?

- No, I haven't checked (0)
- I think there might be, but I'm not certain (1)
- Yes, I'm aware of any that apply (2)

**Q4.4** Could you currently explain, in order, what has happened so far in this dispute?

- No, not clearly (0)
- Roughly, but with gaps (1)
- Yes, clearly (2)

### Dimension 5: Options Awareness

**Q5.1** Are you aware that disputes like this can generally be handled in more than one way (e.g. direct negotiation, mediation, formal process)?

- No (0)
- I have a general awareness (1)
- Yes (2)

**Q5.2** Do you broadly understand the different types of ways a dispute may be managed or progressed?

- No (0)
- I have a general idea (1)
- Yes (2)

**Q5.3** Are you aware that different options may have different implications for cost, time, and relationships?

- No (0)
- I have some awareness (1)
- Yes (2)

**Q5.4** Do you know, broadly, where you could go to get further guidance on the options available to you?

- No (0)
- I have some idea (1)
- Yes (2)

### Dimension 6: Preparedness

**Q6.1** If you needed to explain this situation to someone else today (an adviser, a colleague, a board), how ready would you feel?

- Not ready at all (0)
- Somewhat ready (1)
- Well prepared (2)

**Q6.2** Do you feel you currently have what you need to decide your next step?

- No (0)
- Partly (1)
- Yes (2)

**Q6.3** Are there important aspects of the situation that are still unclear to you?

- Yes, several (0)
- Yes, some (1)
- No, the main points are clear to me (2)

**Q6.4** Overall, how in control of this situation do you currently feel?

- Not very in control (0)
- Somewhat in control (1)
- In control (2)

## 5. Non-scored complexity / risk screening questions

Context only. Do not affect scoring.

- Have formal proceedings or a formal process already begun? (Yes/No)
- Is there a known deadline or time-sensitive requirement? (Yes/No)
- Could the financial, property, or business consequences be significant? (Yes/No)
- Are multiple parties or organisations involved? (Yes/No)
- Are professional advisers already involved? (Yes/No)

If one or more flags trigger, fixed sentence appended:

> "Your responses also indicate one or more factors that may increase the complexity or significance of this matter."

No elaboration. No automatic recommendation follows.

## 6. Output structure

1. **Dispute Readiness Profile** — six dimensions, each with its band only, no scores/percentages
2. **Complexity note** — only if a flag triggered, fixed wording as above
3. **Closing routes** — always shown, never weighted by result:
   - Explore Cosil Membership — for ongoing community, insight and discussion as your situation develops
   - Discuss individual support — for Cosil to look specifically at your circumstances

## 7. Output wording requirement

All band descriptions, complexity wording and result text must use fixed, neutral, non-advisory templates. No result text may vary in a way that implies a recommended next action.

## 8. What this product must never do

- Never scores or comments on legal merit or likelihood of success
- Never recommends a specific route, argument, or action
- Never drafts or suggests wording for correspondence
- Never tells the person what evidence would prove their case
- Never implies a strong profile means professional help isn't needed
- Never implies a weak profile means they must instruct Cosil
